Julio Galindo-Gutiérrez

Postdoctoral Research Fellow
Department of Environment and Geography
University of York

I research how financial institutions, markets, and regulatory frameworks shape the transition toward sustainable and climate-aligned economies. My work examines the political economy of climate finance, the role of capital markets in sustainable development, and environmental politics more broadly. I am particularly interested in how central banks, investors, and supranational regulators seek to align financial incentives with climate commitments. More specifically, my research analyzes how the institutional design of regulatory standards, monetary policy, and development finance frameworks influences pricing, risk perception, and access to sustainable finance.

My articles have been published in a number of leading peer-reviewed journals including the Review of International Political Economy, Journal of International Relations and Development, and Party Politics.
